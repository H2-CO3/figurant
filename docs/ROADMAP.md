# Roadmap

Where Figurant is going. Nothing here is promised for a date.

## Done

- The engine: a body with arms and legs for any head, a clock, actors,
  props, a director that places characters on a page, scenes of several
  characters on tracks in time. Scene format frozen at version 1.
- The atelier: one page, one box to talk in, a conversation by files with a
  coding assistant, a timeline to seek, versions from git, styles, export to
  GIF and to a folder for a website.
- The whiteboard: draw, give the drawing legs, or have a character made of it.
- A library in six styles.

## Next

- **An MCP server** beside the conversation by files, so that an assistant
  drives the page and hears about it without polling.
- **One command to install** (npm), and `figurant` on the path.
- **The timeline moves clips**: drag one to change when it starts.
- **Sections a project declares**: a button and a page of its own in the
  atelier, with the conversation, the versions and the export, without
  touching the engine.
- **Sound**: sound files on a scene's tracks and on an animation's gestures,
  played in step with the clock, and an export to video. Figurant plays the
  files; making them is the assistant's job, with whatever sound tool the
  person has.
- **Variants**: try another take of an animation next to the first.
- GIFs of a character alone and of a prop.
- Names shown in the person's language.
- The atelier's stylesheet was written in layers: about a fifth of it is
  rules overridden further down. To be rewritten as one sheet.

## Later

- A browser extension that puts a character on any page.
- Play: characters that answer the keyboard, for small games.
